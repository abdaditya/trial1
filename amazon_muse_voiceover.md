# Amazon just kicked Meta's AI out of its store — Voiceover

**Length:** about 245 words, roughly 90–95 seconds (the Google × Wiz pace).

---

Meta built an AI that shops for you.
Amazon kicked it out in less than two weeks.

It's called Muse.
Meta launched it on September 8th, and in just ten days it became the number one free app in America, ahead of ChatGPT.

You tell Muse what you want. It finds the product, compares prices, and checks out for you.
No scrolling. No searching. You never even open the Amazon app.

And that is exactly the problem.

Picture Amazon as the world's biggest mall.
The moment you walk in and search for something, the first shelves you see are paid for. Brands pay Amazon just to be seen.
That's almost seventy billion dollars a year in ads.

But an AI agent doesn't look at ads.
It walks straight to the product, buys it, and leaves.

So Amazon locked the door.
Muse users got a popup: unauthorized AI agent.

Amazon says Muse never identified itself.
Meta says it never sees anyone's password.

But Amazon runs its own shopping agent. It's called Buy for Me, and it shops on other brands' websites. If a brand doesn't want that, it has to opt out.

Then Shopify did the opposite.
It opened its doors to Muse, and takes a cut of every checkout.
Its stock jumped fifteen percent in two days.

Amazon has already blocked shopping agents from Google, OpenAI and Perplexity.
It even took Perplexity to court, and in August, it lost.

Because the next shopping war isn't about who has the cheapest product.
It's about whose AI you ask first.

Follow for the real stories behind the biggest deals.

---

**Fact notes (for on-screen text and your reference):**

- **Muse launch:** 8 Sep 2026 in the US, on iOS, Android, web (muse.ai) and WhatsApp.
  - Free tier, plus Power at $20/month and Max at $100/month.
  - It checks out using one-time cards generated by Stripe's Link.
  - It became the #1 free iPhone app in the US within 10 days, overtaking ChatGPT.
- **The block:** Amazon blocked Muse on Sunday, 20 Sep 2026.
  - Popup text: "Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use."
  - Amazon's reasons: Muse doesn't identify itself, and it "appears to capture and store customer credentials."
  - Meta's reply: Muse "has no visibility into people's passwords or payment methods."
- **Amazon's ad business:** over $68 billion in 2025. Q2 2026 alone was $19.8 billion, up 26%.
  - About 90% of that comes from sponsored product listings, which is exactly what an agent skips.
  - AI-referred shoppers converted about 40% better than search visitors on Prime Day 2026.
- **Amazon's own agents:**
  - Rufus: 300 million users and about $12 billion in extra sales in 2025.
  - Buy for Me: shops on third-party brand sites, and brands have to opt out.
- **Shopify:** announced it was opening up to Muse on 21–22 Sep.
  - Agent orders run through Shop Pay and Shopify Payments, so Shopify earns its normal processing fee on each one.
  - The stock rose about 7% and then another 7%, roughly 15% over two days.
- **Other agents Amazon has blocked:** Google, OpenAI and Perplexity.
  - Amazon won a preliminary injunction against Perplexity in March 2026.
  - The Ninth Circuit reversed it in August 2026, ruling that it's users, not AI companies, who access the site.
