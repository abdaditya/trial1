# Google × Wiz: voiceover script (English, storytelling, target ~1:20)

242 words, roughly 1:20–1:25 at a normal TTS pace. It's built as a story: a cold-open hook, then the origin, then the problem, the product, the proof, the "no", the comeback, and the insight.

## Script (paste into TTS as one paragraph)

In 2024, Google offered four friends 23 billion dollars... and they said no. Months later, Google came back with 32. To understand why, go back to 2001. Four Israeli teenagers meet on a bus to army induction. They serve in elite intelligence units, build their first startup, and sell it to Microsoft for 320 million dollars. Inside Microsoft, they spot a problem nobody is solving. Companies are moving everything to the cloud, renting space on Amazon, Microsoft and Google's computers. Thousands of rooms, thousands of doors... and nobody can see which one is left open. So in 2020, they quit and build Wiz. Connect it, and within minutes it maps your entire cloud, showing exactly which chain of small mistakes leads a hacker to your most valuable data. Then they prove it. In 2021, Wiz's researchers break into Microsoft's own cloud database, with access that could reach thousands of customers, including Fortune 500 companies. Every security chief on earth notices. Wiz hits 100 million dollars in yearly revenue in 18 months, the fastest startup ever. That's why they can say no to Google. The CEO writes: saying no to such humbling offers is tough. Eight months later, Google returns. 32 billion, all cash, the biggest deal in its history. Why? Google is third in the cloud race, and Wiz guards Amazon's and Microsoft's clouds too. Google didn't just buy a security company... it bought a seat inside its rivals' biggest customers. Follow BehindVentures.
