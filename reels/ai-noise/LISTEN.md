# Ear check: 6 moments to listen to (headphones, then phone speaker)

The QA measures sync, loudness and silences, but it can't judge taste. Please listen to these:

| Time | What should happen | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Title types on over TV static | Key clicks feel like a real keyboard, not too loud over the hiss |
| 0:02.3 | Title pushes through the lens | Whoosh + sub drop feel like one motion |
| 0:06–0:17 | Pixels search for the picture | Shepard riser keeps rising without getting shrill; it **cuts dead** just before 0:17 |
| 0:17 | The sunset snaps clean | The braam hits hard after the silence (the hero moment) |
| 0:22–0:48 | Training section | The clock tick slowly speeds up (Dunkirk feel), never annoying; smash to silence at 0:48 |
| 1:00–1:03 | Lighthouse finishes, thunder | Big hit, then two thunder cracks matching the two lightning flashes |

Sounds: Kenney CC0 packs (keys, ticks, metal/bell/soft impacts, low explosions). Score, braams, whooshes, risers and thunder were designed and synthesized for this reel (see `audio/mix.py`).
