# Why Nvidia paid $13 billion for Hugging Face — Voiceover

**Length:** about 290 words, roughly 1:35–1:40 at your usual pace.

---

Nvidia just paid thirteen billion dollars for a company that makes almost no money.

Its name is Hugging Face.
Think of it as the GitHub of AI.
Over eighteen million developers use it, and it hosts more than three million AI models.

But it earns only about a hundred and fifty million dollars a year.
So Nvidia paid around eighty-six times its revenue.

And here's the twist.
In 2023, Hugging Face was worth four and a half billion.
Last year, Nvidia offered to invest at seven billion, and Hugging Face said no. It wanted to stay independent.
This summer, its CEO called Jensen Huang himself.

To understand why, you need to know that the AI world has split in two.

On one side, locked models: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini.
You can't download them. You rent them from one company, and you pay every time you use them.

On the other side, free models: DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama, Mistral.
Anyone can download them and run them on their own computers.
And they're catching up fast.
And almost all of them live on Hugging Face.

Now here's Jensen's problem.
His biggest customers are building their own chips.
Google has its own. So do Amazon and OpenAI. Even Anthropic is building one.
If the locked side wins, those giants run AI on their own chips, not Nvidia's.

But free models run anywhere.
Every startup, every bank, every country that downloads one needs its own chips to run it. And most of them buy Nvidia.

So now Nvidia owns the front door of free AI.
It can see which models the world is downloading before anyone else.

Jensen says it's worth every single penny.

Because Nvidia doesn't need to win the AI race.
It just needs everyone to keep running.

---

**Fact notes (for on-screen text and your reference):**

- **The deal:**
  - Announced 3 September 2026: about $12.93 billion. That's roughly $11.9 billion in cash plus up to $1 billion in retention equity for staff.
  - It's Nvidia's second-biggest acquisition ever. It still needs US antitrust review (FTC/DOJ).
- **Revenue multiple:** Hugging Face makes about $150 million a year, so the price is about 86× revenue.
- **Valuation history:**
  - $4.5 billion in 2023, from a Series D round backed by Google, Amazon, Nvidia, Salesforce and others.
  - In 2025, Hugging Face turned down a roughly $500 million Nvidia investment that would have valued it at $7 billion.
- **Who approached whom:** CEO Clem Delangue approached Huang a few weeks before the deal. He told CNBC that open-source AI "was at a turning point" and needed "more resources, more scale, more visibility."
- **Hugging Face scale:** 18 million+ developers, 3 million+ models, 500,000+ datasets, 1 million+ apps, 200,000+ companies.
- **Free models:**
  - Qwen (Alibaba) is now the community standard, with 151,000+ models built on top of it. That's 2.6× Meta's Llama footprint.
  - Chinese labs released free models of up to 2.78 trillion parameters in 2026 (Hugging Face, State of Open Models, Summer 2026).
- **Big customers making their own chips:**
  - Google has TPUs and Amazon has Trainium.
  - OpenAI's own chip, "Jalapeño", has reached tape-out.
  - Anthropic began co-designing its own chip in August 2026.
  - Custom chips can cut costs by up to 65% versus Nvidia GPUs.
- **Nvidia on Hugging Face:**
  - Nvidia was already the biggest contributor, with 500+ models and 250+ datasets.
  - Huang promised "NVIDIA compute will not be required" to use the platform.
- **Huang's quote (CNBC):** "$12.9 billion is what it took to close the deal, and it's worth every single penny."
- **Market reaction:** Nvidia stock rose only about 0.9% on the day.
