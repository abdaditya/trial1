# The microdrama paywall war (v6)

**The insight the reel is built on:** the business is splitting into two models, and China already showed which one wins.

- **Paid** (ReelShort, Kuku): 50 cents per episode, but so much of the revenue goes on ads that even $785M in revenue loses money.
- **Free** (ByteDance's Hongguo): you pay with ads instead. It overtook Alibaba's Youku, and free became ~80% of China's market.

India is now running the same fight: Kuku (paid, heading for an IPO) against JioHotstar's Tadka (free, 100M users in months).

**Length:** ~280 words, about 1:40. The episode title cards are on screen only; the voiceover never says "episode".

## Voiceover

In China, a free app full of one-minute soap operas has overtaken one of the country's biggest streaming platforms.
And the same war has now started in India.
The microdrama business, explained.

*[EP 1 · THE TRICK]*
It started with a simple trick. Ninety-second episodes, each ending on a cliffhanger.
The first ten are free. Then, right before the slap, the story freezes.
Sixty coins to see what happens.
Finish one show, and you've paid twenty to thirty dollars. For something shot in a week.

*[EP 2 · THE HIDDEN BILL]*
But there's a bill nobody sees.
To find those viewers, the apps buy ads. Endlessly.
ReelShort, the biggest one, spends fifty-five cents of every dollar on marketing. Smaller apps, up to eighty.
So even with seven hundred and eighty-five million dollars in revenue, ReelShort was still estimated to be losing money.

*[EP 3 · CHINA FLIPS IT]*
Then China flipped the game.
ByteDance, TikTok's owner, launched Hongguo and made every episode free.
You don't pay with money. You pay with ads.
It hit two hundred and twelve million users a month and overtook Alibaba's Youku.
Free shows were expected to take eighty percent of China's market.
The paywall collapsed.

*[EP 4 · INDIA]*
Now the same fight is in India.
Kuku bet on paid. Its revenue grew almost six times, to fourteen hundred crore, and it's heading for an IPO.
Then in April, JioHotstar launched Tadka. Completely free.
It crossed a hundred million users within months.

*[EP 5 · THE REAL WINNER]*
And when the show is free, the winner is whoever makes it cheapest.
With AI, a show that cost twenty lakh rupees now costs two.

*[FINALE]*
So who wins India?
The app that charges you fifty cents, or the one that sells your attention to advertisers?

Follow for the real stories behind the biggest deals.

## Fact notes

- **Hongguo (ByteDance):**
  - 212M monthly active users in June 2025, overtaking Youku's 200M. It was the first microdrama app to pass a long-form video platform.
  - Free (ad-supported) shows passed paid by December 2024 and were expected to be ~80% of the market in 2025.
  - It has paid 2B+ yuan to production partners since June 2024 (Caixin, Aug 2025).
- **Overseas apps:** 300+ apps, most spending up to 80% of revenue on marketing, and 90%+ predicted to fail (Caixin).
- **ReelShort:**
  - Marketing was about 55% of 2025 revenue.
  - Revenue was $785M in 2025, with an estimated ~$12M loss.
  - Pricing: about 8–13 episodes free, then 60 coins per episode; about 50 episodes cost close to $30 (ReelShort fan site).
- **Production:** about $150k–250k per series, shot in about a week.
- **Kuku:** revenue went ₹240 Cr → ₹1,400 Cr (FY25 → FY26), and it has filed a confidential DRHP.
- **Tadka:**
  - Launched April 4, 2026, free and ad-supported, with 100+ shows at launch (IndianTelevision).
  - Reached 100M users in 2026 (Variety / The Hollywood Reporter).
- **AI:** a show costs ₹20–25 lakh normally, or ₹2–3 lakh with AI (The Hollywood Reporter India).
- **Extra fact (optional cut-in):** China's Hengdian studios, the world's biggest film lot, had 600+ microdrama crews shooting in Q1 2025 and is nicknamed "Vertical-dian" (The World of Chinese).
